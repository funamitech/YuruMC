Installing a modpack

Both packs are ordinary modpack files, so any launcher that imports them will do. The vanilla launcher will not. This is the one part of YuruMC that needs more than an address.

  1. 1

    Grab a launcher

    Anything that can import a pack file: the Modrinth App or Prism Launcher for .mrpack, the CurseForge app or Prism for the CurseForge zip.

  2. 2

    Import the pack

    Our pack: the .mrpack from the mirror. Modded Survival: {{pack.modded-survival.name}} {{pack.modded-survival.version}} from CurseForge. The loader ships inside the pack, so you never install (Neo)Forge yourself.

  3. 3

    Add the server & go

    Launch the pack, add modded.mc.funami.tech or survival.modded.mc.funami.tech, leave the port empty. Enjoy!

The two rules from the front page apply here too: don't lag the server, don't grief. Modded gives you far more creative ways to lag a server. Please be gentle; it lives in someone's home, not a datacenter.
