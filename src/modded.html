We fiddled with it slightly

The server runs stock TerraFirmaGreg-Modern 0.13.10 with three small edits: cubes-without-borders is left out, and wakes-reforged plus particular-reforged are added, because boats should make waves. Client-side either way — install the pack as published and you are fine.

Which one do I want?

They are different versions, different loaders and different vibes, so you cannot mix them up. Yui tried.

Pick YuruMC Modded if…

…you want the kitchen sink. 229 mods, magic and machines and several other dimensions, on Minecraft 1.21.1. One download from our mirror and you are in. Fair warning: it's adventure mode, so your first act on the server will be asking an op for permission to punch a tree.

There is a live map, so your friends can watch you fail from a browser.

Pick Modded Survival if…

…you think vanilla survival is too easy and you would like to be humbled by geology. TerraFirmaGreg-Modern on Minecraft 1.20.1, Forge. Bring patience; there is no wood pickaxe waiting for you.

No live map. The wilderness stays wild.

Installing a modpack

Both packs are ordinary modpack files, so any launcher that imports them will do. Vanilla launcher will not — this is the one part of YuruMC that needs more than an address.

  1. 1

    Grab a launcher

    Anything that can import a pack file — the Modrinth App or Prism Launcher for .mrpack, the CurseForge app or Prism for the CurseForge zip.

  2. 2

    Import the pack

    Our pack: the .mrpack from the mirror. Modded Survival: TerraFirmaGreg-Modern 0.13.10 from CurseForge. The loader comes with it — you do not install NeoForge yourself.

  3. 3

    Add the server & go

    Launch the pack, add modded.mc.funami.tech or survival.modded.mc.funami.tech, leave the port empty. Enjoy!

The two rules from the front page apply here too: don't lag the server, don't grief. Modded gives you *far* more creative ways to lag a server. Please be gentle; it lives in someone's home, not a datacenter.
